Our Four Safety Standards

  • 1:2 Maximum Ratio: Only 2 rope guests per certified guide on high-consequence glacial arêtes.
  • Dual Satellite Redundancy: Constant Garmin inReach satellite breadcrumbs and emergency SOS tethering.
  • Integrated Rescue Coverage: Helicopter dispatch insurance included in every expedition fee.
  • Professional Acclimatization Schedules: Calculated ascent gradients to eliminate high-altitude sickness risks.

Alpine Stewardship Since 2012

The Nexeus Alpine Philosophy

We believe the purest communion with wilderness happens above the clouds, where clean stone, perpetual ice, and silence reign.

Leave No Trace

We pack out 100% of waste from alpine bivouacs and fund trail restoration efforts in the Swiss Valais and French Haute-Savoie.

Small-Group Intimacy

No industrial tours or noisy crowds. We limit teams to small groups to preserve the sanctity of the high mountains.

Local Alpine Culture

We partner exclusively with family-owned mountain refuges, valley farmers, and local mountain guides clubs.
